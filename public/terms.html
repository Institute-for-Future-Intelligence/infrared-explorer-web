1. What the Service is, and is not

Infrared Explorer is a scientific instrument for thermal imaging with a FLIR ONE camera attached to your phone, and a citizen-science tool for capturing and sharing thermal street views. It includes AI features that help you plan an experiment, analyze what you recorded, and draft a report (section 9). It is intended for science education, scientific exploration, and raising awareness of energy efficiency.

It is not a medical device. Do not use it to measure body temperature or for any medical, health, or veterinary purpose. Temperature readings depend on the camera, its calibration and warm-up, the emissivity of surfaces, distance, and ambient conditions; they are estimates for learning and exploration and must not be relied on for safety-critical, regulatory, commercial, or professional decisions without independent verification.

Class members can see one another’s names and email addresses on the roster, as described in the Privacy Policy.
